Featured project

M.S. thesis · IEEE SSRR 2024

Two robots, one person to follow

A slow, sensor-rich ground robot and a fast micro-drone share the job of following a person through cluttered spaces where GPS and overhead views aren't available. I designed the coordination algorithm, compared it against multi-agent RL and a single-robot baseline, and validated it on real hardware.

9/9 paths completed 1.95× faster than RL 89% success on the hardest paths
